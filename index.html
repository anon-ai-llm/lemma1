Show the mathematics

100 states ordered by Xj. The policy sends the top 100·p* of them to h+ (ones) and the rest to h− (zeros): Y = h− + (h+ − h−)·1{Xj > θ*} with p* = Pr(Xj > θ*). The split takes the top 100·p as its cell S = {Xj > θ}, p = Pr(S); the rest is Sc. Each bucket's mean is its fraction of ones; the leaf values are those means.

Closed forms: for p ≤ p*, γ(p)/(h+−h−) = (1−p*)/(1−p); for p ≥ p*, p*/p. The gain p(1−p)γ2 is largest only at p = p*, where both buckets are pure, γ = h+−h−, and the gain equals p*(1−p*) = Var(Y)/(h+−h−)2.
